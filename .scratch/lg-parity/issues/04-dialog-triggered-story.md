## What to build

Add a Triggered Story for MUI Dialog at `Components/Feedback/Dialog`. The story includes a Button that opens and closes the dialog — the dialog should not render in its open state by default. The wrapper exposes dialog-level props as controls.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `Dialog.tsx` wrapper exists under `src/Components/`
- [ ] `Dialog.stories.tsx` filed at `Components/Feedback/Dialog`
- [ ] Story renders a Button trigger that opens and closes the dialog
- [ ] 2–4 props (e.g. `fullWidth`, `maxWidth`, `fullScreen`) wired as interactive controls
- [ ] Renders correctly under the House Theme in light and dark mode
